DEFINITION OF RATINGS
(5) EXCEPTIONAL: Consistently exceeds all relevant performance standards.
(4) EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS: Consistently meets and often exceeds all relevant performance standards.
(3) MEETS EXPECTATIONS: Meets all relevant performance standards.
(2) BELOW EXPECTATIONS: Sometimes meets the performance standards.
(1) NEEDS IMPROVEMENT: Consistently falls short of performance standards.
Describe the employee's contributions in each of the performance categories below. It is COMPULSORY that you illustrate specific, detailed examples. Ratings MUST be supported and substantiated by narrative comments.
I. TECHNICAL / JOB KNOWLEDGE
Competency
I.1 Job Knowledge *
Possesses skills and knowledge to perform the job competently. Has used his technical knowledge to generate improvements in processes, procedures, and operations.
I.2 Job Performance and Quality of Work *
Completes and performs all the requirements of his job. Handles every technical challenge skillfully, thoroughly, and effectively. Provides high-quality work and thoroughly follows standards and procedures. Keeps complete records.
I.3 Productivity *
Producing required output on time; Was able to meet deadlines for his/her job.
I.4 Ability to Follow Instruction/Direction of Superior *
Knows, understand and follow what is being instructed by immediate superior.
II. BEHAVIORAL ATTRIBUTES
Competency
II.1 Cost Consciousness *
Maximizing the use of office resources and saving the company from unnecessary cost imperative. Has applied his/her expertise to save the company a substantial amount of money.
II.2 Communication and Customer Focus (Sales, HR, Procurement) *
Organizes and expresses ideas and information clearly and is capable of conveying ideas and information to others. Consistently fully meets the needs of internal and external customers.
II.3 Teamwork, Conflict Resolution & Attitude towards Colleagues *
Demonstrates ability to get along with others, is respectful of co-workers, communicates and acts as a team player, promotes teamwork, responds and acts appropriately to confrontational situations.
II.4 Flexibility and Works Under Pressure *
Demonstrates adaptability and flexibility with changes in jobs and duties. Adapts quickly to change and easily considers new approaches. Thinks and acts effectively under pressure. Overcomes obstacles to achieve results. Willingness to accept additional tasks not part of his/her regular duties.
II.5 Initiative & Motivation *
Strives to learn and improve. Seeks out ways to better himself/herself and the company. Takes on responsibilities. Does not remain idle. Is hard working and self-motivated. Has a drive within him/herself to do a better job, be a better employee and seeks opportunities to improve or learn more.
